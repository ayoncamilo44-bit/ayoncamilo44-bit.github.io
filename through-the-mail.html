The good kind of slow

Make room for a real letter.

Through the Mail collecting is part research, part patience, and part delight. There’s no algorithm for the feeling of finding a signed return in your mailbox.

Tip: A clear, kind request and a self-addressed stamped envelope go a long way.
01 — DISCOVER

Choose a signer

Find someone whose work means something to you and learn a little about their story.

02 — PREPARE

Write with care

Keep your note personal, concise, and easy to answer. Include what you’d love signed.

03 — SEND

Trust the mailbox

Address your envelope carefully, send it off, and let the anticipation do its thing.
